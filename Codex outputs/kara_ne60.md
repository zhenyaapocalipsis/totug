The thirteen crossed-out cards and Elemental Cataclysm were replaced. Groff uses the Elemental Cataclysm slot by the owner’s choice. The half-deck contains 40 cards across 20 slots, with the same IDs, costs, aspects, abilities and victory points.

The artwork comes from the owner's folder. Ivana Boritsi uses Irina Nordsol’s illustration from *Van Richten’s Guide to Ravenloft*. Source names and crop coordinates are recorded in [kara_ne60_changes.json](kara_ne60_changes.json).

D&D Beyond lists **Groff**, **Blob of Annihilation**, **Gulthias Blight**, and **Treefolk**. The last two remained in the candidate pool. [Strixhaven](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/sacoc), [Monster Manual](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/mm-2024), [Eldraine creatures](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters?filter-search=&filter-type=15&page=3&sort=-size).

The three Hearthstone assignments for Azer Sentinel, Elemental Cataclysm and Khargra are available again. The five new single-copy cards are ready for new assignments.

Preview sheets include full-size cards and miniatures in [English](kara_ne60_en.png) and [Russian](kara_ne60_ru.png).
